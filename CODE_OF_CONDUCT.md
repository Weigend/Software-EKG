# Code of Conduct

**English** | [Deutsch](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.de.md)

We want to enable collaboration that is factual, respectful and welcoming.

- Treat others with respect and assume good intentions.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Accept different experiences, perspectives and levels of knowledge.
- Do not publish other people's private information, and avoid harassment, insults or
  discriminatory behaviour.

Project maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that contradict these principles.
Incidents can be reported confidentially via the contact channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). This is not a substitute for emergency services or law enforcement.
